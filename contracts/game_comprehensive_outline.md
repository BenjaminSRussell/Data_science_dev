
# Data Science Tycoon - Comprehensive Game Outline

This document outlines the entire game structure, including menu options, visual styles, shop items, characters, and gameplay mechanics.

---

# 1. Main Menu / UI Structure
**Visual Style:** Modern, clean, "Dark Mode" IDE aesthetic (VS Code / Terminal vibes).
**Navigation:** Sidebar or Top Bar with the following tabs:
*   **Workstation:** The core gameplay loop (Contract selection & coding).
*   **Shop:** Amazon-style marketplace for upgrades.
*   **Map:** City view for networking and story events.
*   **Management:** Office upgrades and Staff hiring.
*   **Inventory:** View unlocked items and current stats.

---

# 2. Workstation (The Core Loop)
**Visual:** A virtual desktop with an email client or "Job Board" app.
**Action:** Select a contract to begin the coding minigame.

## Contract Categories (Selectable via Tabs)
*   **Standard Contracts:** Basic visualization tasks. (Low Risk, Regular Pay)
    *   *Examples:* Quarterly Sales Report, Website Traffic Overview, Employee Satisfaction Survey.
*   **Data Science Projects:** Advanced analysis tasks. (High Intellect Requirement)
    *   *Examples:* Customer Churn Analysis, Fraud Detection Model, Predictive Maintenance.
*   **Client Tiers (Unlockable):**
    *   Small Business ($50-$200)
    *   Startup ($150-$500)
    *   Marketing Agency ($200-$600)
    *   Enterprise ($500-$2,000)
    *   Government ($400-$1,500)
    *   Fortune 500 ($2,000-$10,000)

---

# 3. The Shop (Upgrades & Perks)
**Visual:** E-commerce interface with product cards, ratings, and "Buy" buttons.

## Tools (One-time purchases)
*   **Data Filter ($300):** Filter data before visualization.
*   **Advanced Sort ($250):** Sort data by any column.
*   **Data Aggregation ($600):** Combine and summarize data points.
*   **Chart Annotations ($400):** Add notes to charts.
*   **High-Res Export ($350):** Export charts in various formats.

## Software Licenses (Passive Buffs)
*   **IDE Pro License ($250):** Reduced bugs, faster coding.
*   **AutoML Suite ($800):** Automate model selection.
*   **Cloud Compute Credits ($400):** Offload training to the cloud.
*   **Enterprise DB License ($1,200):** Handle massive datasets.
*   **Neural Architecture Search ($2,500):** AI designing AI.

## Perks (Consumables/Permanent Buffs)
*   **Time Extension ($700):** +30 seconds on all tasks.
*   **Office Coffee ($500):** Bosses are 10% more lenient.
*   **Negotiation Skills ($1,000):** +15% money from all tasks.
*   **Networking ($800):** +20% reputation from tasks.
*   **Second Chance ($1,500):** Retry one task per day.
*   **Data Insight ($600):** See hints for optimal chart type.

# 4. Management (Tycoon Elements)
**Visual:** Isometric view of your current office or a dashboard of stats.

## Office Locations (Progression)
1.  **Bedroom Corner ($0):** "Humble beginnings." (Capacity: 1)
2.  **Home Office ($2,000):** "A dedicated room." (Capacity: 1, +10% Client Bonus)
3.  **Co-working Space ($5,000):** "Network with others!" (Capacity: 2, +20% Client Bonus)
4.  **Small Office ($15,000):** "Your first real office!" (Capacity: 4, +35% Client Bonus)
5.  **Office Floor ($50,000):** "A whole floor." (Capacity: 10, +50% Client Bonus)
6.  **Company HQ ($200,000):** "Your own building." (Capacity: 25, +75% Client Bonus)

## Manage Staff (Hiring Menu)
*   **Intern ($50/day):** Eager to learn, needs supervision.
*   **Junior Analyst ($150/day):** Can handle basic charts independently.
*   **Data Analyst ($300/day):** Solid all-around performer.
*   **Senior Analyst ($500/day):** Expert-level work, can mentor others.
*   **Data Scientist ($800/day):** ML, statistics, the works.
*   **Project Manager ($400/day):** Boosts client satisfaction by 20%.
*   **Sales Rep ($350/day):** Generates +1 client lead per day.

## Office Equipment (Customization)
*   **Computer:** Old Laptop -> Gaming Rig -> Server Cluster.
*   **Desk:** Folding Table -> Standing Desk -> Executive Desk.
*   **Monitor:** Laptop Screen -> Ultrawide -> Triple 4K Setup.
*   **Chair:** Kitchen Chair -> Gaming Chair -> Herman Miller.

# 5. The Map (World & Characters)
**Visual:** A stylized city map with clickable points of interest. Icons change based on time of day.

## Locations
*   **Home/Office:** Your base of operations.
*   **Tech Hub:** Startup incubator. Meet **Marcus Thompson** (Data Architect) and **Taylor Morgan** (Dev).
*   **University:** Academic center. Meet **Professor Higgins** and **Dr. Amara Patel**.
*   **Downtown:** Financial district. Meet **James Wilson** (Consultant) and **Sophia Zhang** (Investor).
*   **Coffee Shop:** Social hub. Meet **Bob Bagel**, **Rachel Green**, and **Casey Lee**.
*   **Library:** Quiet study area. Meet **Emma Bloom** (Librarian/Romance) and **Luna** (Bookstore Owner).
*   **Gym:** Wellness center. Meet **Jordan Kim** and **Dr. Wellness**.
*   **Government/City Hall:** Legal hub. Meet **Priya Sharma** (Privacy) and **Agent Smith** (IRS).

## Character Interactions
*   **Mentors:** Unlock new skills and stat boosts (Experience).
*   **Investors:** Provide funding in exchange for equity/results.
*   **Rivals:** Challenge you to "Code Battles" or steal clients.
*   **Romance:** Optional dating simulation elements (e.g., Emma Bloom, Bella Lux).
*   **Shady Contacts:** High risk/reward illegal jobs (Vinnie Shark, The Broker).

---

# 6. Mechanics & Systems
*   **Time of Day:** Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Late Night. Affects NPC availability and background visuals.
*   **Weather:** Clear, Rainy, Snowy. Purely cosmetic ambience.
*   **Stats:**
    *   **Python/SQL:** Coding speed and accuracy.
    *   **Statistics:** Model performance.
    *   **Reputation:** Unlocks better clients.
    *   **Ethics:** Affects endings and available contracts (High Ethics = Government, Low Ethics = Shady).
*   **Desk Items:** Collectibles earned from achievements (Mugs, Trophies, Plants).
