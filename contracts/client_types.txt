
# Client Types & Contract Categories
This file lists the different types of clients that offer contracts, their pay ranges, and expectations.

## Small Business
*   **Pay Range:** $50 - $200
*   **Data Complexity:** Low (Level 1)
*   **Patience:** High (0.8)
*   **Description:** "Mom and pop shops need simple reports."
*   **Ideal for:** Learning the basics, quick cash.

## Startup
*   **Pay Range:** $150 - $500
*   **Data Complexity:** Medium (Level 2)
*   **Patience:** Low (0.5)
*   **Description:** "Fast-moving, need quick turnaround."
*   **Ideal for:** Building reputation fast, if you can handle the stress.

## Marketing Agency
*   **Pay Range:** $200 - $600
*   **Data Complexity:** Medium (Level 2)
*   **Patience:** Moderate (0.7)
*   **Description:** "Regular work, fair pay."
*   **Ideal for:** Steady income stream.

## Enterprise
*   **Pay Range:** $500 - $2,000
*   **Data Complexity:** High (Level 3)
*   **Patience:** Good (0.9)
*   **Description:** "Big contracts, complex requirements."
*   **Ideal for:** Experienced analysts, huge portfolio boost.

## Government
*   **Pay Range:** $400 - $1,500
*   **Data Complexity:** High (Level 3)
*   **Patience:** Very High (1.0)
*   **Description:** "Slow but steady, lots of red tape."
*   **Ideal for:** Safe, low-stress income (if you don't mind forms).

## Fortune 500
*   **Pay Range:** $2,000 - $10,000
*   **Data Complexity:** Expert (Level 4)
*   **Patience:** Low (0.6)
*   **Description:** "The big leagues. Don't mess up."
*   **Ideal for:** Becoming a tycoon. High risk, high reward.
