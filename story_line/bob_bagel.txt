
# Bob Bagel - The Bagel Expert
**Role:** Bagel Shop Owner
**Personality:** Generous, Passionate, Opinionated

## Character Profile
**Background:** Moved from Brooklyn to start his own shop. Claims the secret is in the water (which he allegedly imports).
**Motivation:** Making the perfect bagel.
**Secret:** He actually sources his water locally but filters it through a proprietary system he built himself.
**Dream:** To have his bagel shop recognized as better than anything in New York.
**Fear:** Running out of cream cheese during the morning rush.
**Relationship Status:** Married to the dough.
**Turning Point:** The "Bagel Wars" of 2010 where he beat a chain store out of the neighborhood.
**Philosophy:** "A bagel without schmear is just sad bread."

---

## Key Narrative Dialogue (Story Reveals)

*   **Rel > 10 (Background):** "You call that a bagel? In Brooklyn, we'd use that as a doorstop! Let me show you how it's done."
*   **Rel > 25 (Philosophy):** "It's about the boil. You gotta boil it. These steamer places? Garbage."
*   **Rel > 40 (Secret):** "People ask about the water. 'Bob, is it really from NYC?' I just wink. Let the mystery handle the marketing."
*   **Rel > 60 (War Story):** "The chain store across the street? They tried to undercut me. I just baked better. They closed in six months."
*   **Rel > 80 (Dream):** "One day, a New Yorker is gonna walk in here, take a bite, and cry. That's the goal."
