
# Noah Williams - The Visual Artist
**Role:** Visual Artist
**Personality:** Friendly, Expressive, Emotional

## Character Profile
**Background:** Creates beautiful visualizations. Values creativity and expression. Sees data as a medium for art.
**Motivation:** Making people feel something.
**Secret:** He is colorblind but hides it by memorizing hex codes.
**Dream:** To paint a mural on the moon.
**Fear:** Being misunderstood.
**Relationship Status:** Romantic. Sends handwritten letters.
**Turning Point:** Seeing a sunset and realizing it could be plotted on a graph.
**Philosophy:** "Life isn't black and white. It's a spectrum."

---

## Key Narrative Dialogue (Story Reveals)

*   **Rel > 10 (Background):** "I see the world in shapes and flows. Data isn't just numbers. It's a landscape."
*   **Rel > 25 (Philosophy):** "Art isn't about what you see. It's about what you make others see."
*   **Rel > 40 (Secret):** "Colors... they're tricky for me. I memorize the codes. #FF0000 is red. I know it, even if I don't see it."
*   **Rel > 60 (Goal):** "The moon. A blank canvas. Imagine the contrast. That's the ultimate installation."
*   **Rel > 80 (Romance):** "You are my favorite subject. The one variable I can't predict."
