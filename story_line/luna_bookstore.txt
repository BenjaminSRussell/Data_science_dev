
# Luna - The Bookstore Owner
**Role:** Bookstore Owner
**Personality:** Generous, Dreamy, Bibliophile

## Character Profile
**Background:** Inherited an old bookstore and refused to let it die in the digital age. Loves books more than anything.
**Motivation:** preserving the magic of physical books.
**Secret:** She writes fantasy novels under a pen name that are surprisingly popular online.
**Dream:** A library that contains every story ever told.
**Fear:** The death of print.
**Relationship Status:** In love with fictional characters.
**Turning Point:** Finding a first edition of her favorite childhood book in a bargain bin.
**Philosophy:** "A room without books is like a body without a soul."

---

## Key Narrative Dialogue (Story Reveals)

*   **Rel > 10 (Background):** "People say print is dead. I say they just haven't found the right book yet."
*   **Rel > 25 (Philosophy):** "The smell of old paper... it's the smell of history. Of thoughts preserved in time."
*   **Rel > 40 (Secret):** "I live a thousand lives. One in this shop, and 999 others in the pages."
*   **Rel > 60 (Dream):** "I want this place to be a sanctuary. Where the wifi is weak but the coffee is strong."
*   **Rel > 80 (Identity):** "Stories are real. Sometimes, I think they're more real than we are."

---

## Standard Dialogue Progression

### Stranger
*   *Greetings:* "Welcome, traveler.", "Looking for a story?", "Hello."
*   *Topics:* Fiction, non-fiction, the smell of books.

### Friendly
*   *Greetings:* "Ah, a fellow reader.", "Luna here.", "Found anything good?"
*   *Topics:* Recommendations, reading nooks.

### Acquaintance
*   *Greetings:* "Hello! The new arrivals are here.", "Good to see you.", "Read this!"
*   *Topics:* The secret writing hint, literary debates.

### Friend
*   *Greetings:* "My favorite reader!", "Hey! I saved this for you."
*   *Topics:* The sanctuary dream, the "Soul of the Shop".

### Close Friend
*   *Greetings:* "Welcome home.", "Hey! Let's talk plot twists."
*   *Topics:* Sharing her own writing, trust.
