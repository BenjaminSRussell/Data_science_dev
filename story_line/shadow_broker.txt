
# Shadow - The Information Broker
**Role:** Information Broker (Criminal)
**Personality:** Mysterious, Silent, Dangerous

## Character Profile
**Background:** Sells information to the highest bidder. Very secretive. Operates out of the shadows.
**Motivation:** Chaos and Profit.
**Secret:** Operates a network of spy drones disguised as pigeons.
**Dream:** To know everything about everyone.
**Fear:** Silence.
**Relationship Status:** Unknown.
**Turning Point:** Unknown.
**Philosophy:** "Whispers echo louder than shouts."
