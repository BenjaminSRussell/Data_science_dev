
# Agent Smith - The IRS Investigator
**Role:** IRS Investigator
**Personality:** Professional, Stern, Relentless

## Character Profile
**Background:** Investigates financial crimes. Very thorough and by-the-book. Has never lost a case.
**Motivation:** Justice. Order. Taxes.
**Secret:** He collects rare stamps. It's the only time he allows himself to be passionate.
**Dream:** A perfect audit rate.
**Fear:** Chaos.
**Relationship Status:** Married to the Tax Code.
**Turning Point:** Finding a penny error in a billion-dollar ledger that exposed a massive fraud.
**Philosophy:** "Death and Taxes. I handle the latter."

---

## Key Narrative Dialogue (Story Reveals)

*   **Rel > 10 (Background):** "Numbers don't lie. People do. My job is to find the discrepancy between the two."
*   **Rel > 25 (Philosophy):** "Society is a contract. Taxes are the dues. You don't pay, you're stealing from everyone."
*   **Rel > 40 (Secret):** "Stamps. Tiny squares of history. Perfectly perforated. Organized. I find them... soothing."
*   **Rel > 60 (Goal):** "The perfect system. Where every transaction is accounted for. No shadows. No leaks."
*   **Rel > 80 (Respect):** "You keep clean books. I respect that. In my line of work, that's rare."
