
# Tyler Brooks - The Budget Rival
**Role:** Competing Freelancer
**Personality:** Scrappy, Efficient, Fast

## Character Profile
**Background:** Undercuts your rates. Always trying to one-up you. Runs a "volume" business.
**Motivation:** Money. Fast money.
**Secret:** He cuts corners. A lot of corners.
**Dream:** To automate his entire job and move to Bali.
**Fear:** Being sued for negligence.
**Relationship Status:** Single, swiping right on everyone.
**Turning Point:** Discovered outsourcing.
**Philosophy:** "Good, Fast, Cheap. Pick two? Nah, I give 'em Cheap and Fast. Who needs Good?"

## Key Narrative Dialogue (Story Reveals)

*   **Rel > 10 (Background):** "Why charge 500 when you can charge 50 and do ten times the volume? It's math, bro."
*   **Rel > 25 (Philosophy):** "Clients don't read the code. They just want it to run. Usually."
*   **Rel > 40 (Secret):** "I got a guy. A guy who knows a guy. We get it done."
*   **Rel > 60 (Goal):** "Bali. Laptop on the beach. Piña Colada. That's the life."
*   **Rel > 80 (Risk):** "Liability? That's what LLCs are for, right? ...Right?"
