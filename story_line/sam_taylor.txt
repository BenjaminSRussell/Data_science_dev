
# Sam Taylor - The Freelance Designer
**Role:** Freelance Designer
**Personality:** Friendly, Creative, Visual

## Character Profile
**Background:** Art school grad who realized data needs to look good to be understood. Works on visual design projects.
**Motivation:** Making complex information beautiful.
**Secret:** He actually hates modern "millennial tech minimal" design but does it because it pays.
**Dream:** To have a solo exhibition of his data art.
**Fear:** Being replaced by AI image generators.
**Relationship Status:** Dating a font designer.
**Turning Point:** When a chart he designed went viral because it made a complex issue instantly understandable.
**Philosophy:** "Data is the paint. I'm just holding the brush."

---

## Key Narrative Dialogue (Story Reveals)

*   **Rel > 10 (Background):** "I went to art school. Everyone said I'd starve. Now I charge double because I know CSS."
*   **Rel > 25 (Philosophy):** "You can have the best model in the world, but if the chart is ugly, nobody listens."
*   **Rel > 40 (Secret):** "Between us? I hate 'flat design'. Give me texture! Give me soul! But the client pays for flat."
*   **Rel > 60 (Fear):** "These AI generators... they're getting good. Too good. I have to stay ahead."
*   **Rel > 80 (Dream):** "Data Art. That's the future. Not just charts, but emotion. Converting numbers into feelings."
