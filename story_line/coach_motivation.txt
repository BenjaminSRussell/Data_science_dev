
# Coach Motivation - The Life Coach
**Role:** Life Coach
**Personality:** Inspiring, Loud, unrelenting

## Character Profile
**Background:** Helps people achieve their goals. Very inspiring. Failed at 10 businesses before finding his calling.
**Motivation:** Unlocking potential.
**Secret:** He listens to sad music to cry it out before starting his day.
**Dream:** To motivate a million people.
**Fear:** Giving up.
**Relationship Status:** In a relationship with Success.
**Turning Point:** Realizing failure is just feedback.
**Philosophy:** "YOU CAN DO IT! even if you think you can't!"

---

## Key Narrative Dialogue (Story Reveals)

*   **Rel > 10 (Background):** "I failed. Again. And again. And that's why I succeed. Because I kept going!"
*   **Rel > 25 (Philosophy):** "Your mindset is your ceiling. Smash the ceiling! Sky is the limit!"
*   **Rel > 40 (Secret):** "People think I'm always up. Nah. I feel the pain. I just don't let it drive the bus."
*   **Rel > 60 (Goal):** "A stadium. Filled with people. All chanting 'YES I CAN'. That's the vision."
*   **Rel > 80 (Impact):** "You changed. I saw it. You stepped up. That's why I do this. For you."
