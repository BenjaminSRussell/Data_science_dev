
# Judge Roberts - The City Judge
**Role:** City Judge
**Personality:** Professional, Wise, Impartial

## Character Profile
**Background:** Fair and just. Respects those who follow the law. Has served on the bench for 30 years.
**Motivation:** Upholding the pillars of society.
**Secret:** She writes mystery novels where the judge is the villain.
**Dream:** To retire and write full-time.
**Fear:** Making a wrong ruling.
**Relationship Status:** Widowed.
**Turning Point:** Overturning a wrongful conviction. The weight of the gavel never felt heavier.
**Philosophy:** "Justice is blind, but I am not."

---

## Key Narrative Dialogue (Story Reveals)

*   **Rel > 10 (Background):** "The law is a living thing. It breathes. It changes. But the core principles remain."
*   **Rel > 25 (Philosophy):** "It is not enough to do right. One must also be *seen* doing right."
*   **Rel > 40 (Secret):** "I see so many villains. Sometimes, in my stories... I wonder what makes them. Even a judge has imagination."
*   **Rel > 60 (Goal):** "A quiet desk. A typewriter. And a story where I control the ending. That is retirement."
*   **Rel > 80 (Wisdom):** "You walk a straight path. It is harder, but it leads to better places. Keep walking."
