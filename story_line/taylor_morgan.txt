
# Taylor Morgan - The Developer
**Role:** Software Developer
**Personality:** Friendly, Technical, Curious

## Character Profile
**Background:** Full-stack developer who loves the entire stack. Always working on side projects.
**Motivation:** Building cool stuff.
**Secret:** He holds a patent for a toaster that tweets, but he's embarrassed about it.
**Dream:** To launch a SaaS that gives him passive income for life.
**Fear:** Spaghetti code.
**Relationship Status:** In a relationship with GitHub Copilot.
**Turning Point:** Refactoring a legacy codebase into a microservices architecture and seeing the latency drop by 90%.
**Philosophy:** "If it's not on GitHub, did it even happen?"

---

## Key Narrative Dialogue (Story Reveals)

*   **Rel > 10 (Background):** "I started coding on a calculator. TI-83. Snake game. The rest is history."
*   **Rel > 25 (Philosophy):** "Full stack isn't a job, it's a lifestyle. You gotta know the DB *and* the CSS."
*   **Rel > 40 (Secret):** "I have this patent. A tweeting toaster. It seemed like a good idea in 2014. Don't google it."
*   **Rel > 60 (Side Project):** "I'm working on something new. It automates the automation of the build script. Meta, right?"
*   **Rel > 80 (Dream):** "Passive income. That's the goal. Code once, get paid forever. Then? Beach."
