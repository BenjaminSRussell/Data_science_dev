
# Robert Kim - The Seed Investor
**Role:** Seed Investor
**Personality:** Professional, Analytical, Optimistic

## Character Profile
**Background:** Early-stage investor who made his money in the dot-com boom. Focuses on data-driven startups.
**Motivation:** Finding diamonds in the rough. Loves the energy of early-stage founders.
**Secret:** He relies heavily on his "gut feeling" rather than just spreadsheets, which contradicts his analytical persona.
**Dream:** To find a founder who reminds him of his younger self.
**Fear:** Missing out on "The Next Big Thing" (FOMO).
**Relationship Status:** Family man. Often talks about his kids' coding projects.
**Turning Point:** Passing on a startup that became a unicorn. He vowed never to be too cautious again.
**Philosophy:** "I don't invest in ideas. I invest in people."

---

## Key Narrative Dialogue (Story Reveals)

*   **Rel > 10 (Background):** "I was there in the 90s. The Wild West. We built the internet with duct tape and hope."
*   **Rel > 25 (Philosophy):** "Your spreadsheet looks nice. But tell me... why YOU? Why this? Why now?"
*   **Rel > 40 (Secret Method):** "The numbers have to work, yes. But I look for the spark. The fire in the eyes. That's what sells me."
*   **Rel > 60 (Regret):** "I passed on a company once. They're worth billions now. It taught me that sometimes, you have to take the leap."
*   **Rel > 80 (Dream):** "I'm looking for the next generation. Someone with the vision to change things. Are you that person?"

---

*   *Greetings:* "Hello! Any updates?", "Robert here.", "Good to connect."
*   *Topics:* Gut feeling vs Data, dot-com stories.

### Friend
*   *Greetings:* "My friend! How's it going?", "Hey! Let's chat."
*   *Topics:* The fear of missing out, believing in founders.

### Close Friend
*   *Greetings:* "Robert. I trust you.", "Hey! You've got that spark."
*   *Topics:* Mentoring the next generation, partnership.
