
# Sophia Zhang - The Corporate Investor
**Role:** Corporate Investor
**Personality:** Professional, Strategic, Calculating

## Character Profile
**Background:** Representative for a major tech corporation's investment arm. She doesn't just write checks; she looks for strategic acquisitions.
**Motivation:** Expanding the corporation's dominance through acquisition.
**Secret:** She actually admires the freedom of independent startups and sometimes envies the founders she acquires.
**Dream:** To broker the largest tech merger in history.
**Fear:** A bad acquisition that drags the stock price down.
**Relationship Status:** Married to the job.
**Turning Point:** Negotiating a billion-dollar buyout at age 29.
**Philosophy:** "Innovate or buy. We prefer to buy."

---

## Key Narrative Dialogue (Story Reveals)

*   **Rel > 10 (Background):** "I represent... significant interests. We have resources you can only dream of."
*   **Rel > 25 (Philosophy):** "Why compete when you can join us? Scale is everything in this market."
*   **Rel > 40 (Secret Envy):** "You have so much... freedom. No board meetings. No compliance checks. Enjoy it while it lasts."
*   **Rel > 60 (Strategy):** "We don't just want your tech. We want your talent. We want *you*."
*   **Rel > 80 (Dream):** "Imagine what you could do with our resources. No limits. No budget constraints. Just pure innovation."
