
# Carlos - The Tech Store Owner
**Role:** Tech Store Owner (Mall)
**Personality:** Friendly, Knowledgeable, Geeky

## Character Profile
**Background:** Runs the best tech store in town. Knows all the latest gear because he tests it all himself. 
**Motivation:** Helping people build their dream setups.
**Secret:** He mines crypto on the display units at night to pay the rent.
**Dream:** To build a supercomputer in the back room.
**Fear:** Obsolescence.
**Relationship Status:** In a committed relationship with his GPU cluster.
**Turning Point:** Overclocking his first CPU at age 12 without melting it.
**Philosophy:** "Have you tried turning it off and on again? Seriously, it works."

---

## Key Narrative Dialogue (Story Reveals)

*   **Rel > 10 (Background):** "I don't just sell this stuff. I live it. Every GPU on this shelf? I've benchmarked it personally."
*   **Rel > 25 (Philosophy):** "Specs aren't everything. It's about the synergy. A 4090 implies a bottleneck if your CPU is trash."
*   **Rel > 40 (Secret):** "To be honest... the electric bill here is insane. Let's just say the display units work the night shift."
*   **Rel > 60 (Dream):** "Imagine a Beowulf cluster made of gaming consoles. I think I can do it."
*   **Rel > 80 (Advice):** "Hardware is temporary. Skills are forever. But a good mechanical keyboard helps."
