
# Rachel Green - The Product Visionary
**Role:** Product Manager
**Personality:** Friendly, Organized, User-Focused

## Character Profile
**Background:** Manages products at a growing startup. Pivoted from marketing to product because she wanted to "build things, not just sell them."
**Motivation:** creating products that genuinely solve user problems.
**Secret:** She feels like an imposter among technical engineers, despite understanding the market better than any of them.
**Dream:** To launch a product that becomes a household name.
**Fear:** Launching a product that nobody wants.
**Relationship Status:** Dating, but "it's complicated" due to her startup hours.
**Turning Point:** Her first successful feature launch—seeing users actually use and love something she designed.
**Philosophy:** "Data tells you *what* isn't working. Empathy tells you *why*."

---

## Key Narrative Dialogue (Story Reveals)

*   **Rel > 10 (Background):** "I used to be in marketing. But I got tired of spinning stories. I wanted to build the story."
*   **Rel > 25 (Motivation):** "It's not about the features. It's about the feeling. Does this make the user's life easier?"
*   **Rel > 40 (Secret Insecurity):** "Sometimes... I sit in engineering meetings and feel like a fraud. But then I remember they don't know the user like I do."
*   **Rel > 60 (Dream):** "I want to walk down the street and see someone using my app. Just... naturally. That's the dream."
*   **Rel > 80 (Philosophy):** "You can have all the data in the world. But if you don't have empathy, you're building in the dark."

## Standard Dialogue Progression

### Stranger
*   *Greetings:* "Hi there.", "Do you have a minute?", "Hey."
*   *Topics:* Roadmaps, deadlines, user feedback.

### Friendly
*   *Greetings:* "Hey! Good to see you.", "Hi! How's it going?", "Hey."
*   *Topics:* Recent user testing, "feature creep" (annoyance).

### Acquaintance
*   *Greetings:* "Hey! Always good to sync up.", "Hi! Catch up soon?", "What's new?"
*   *Topics:* Balancing business needs vs user wants, startup chaos.

### Friend
*   *Greetings:* "My favorite data person!", "Hey! Coffee?", "So glad you're here."
*   *Topics:* Imposter syndrome, "Relationship status: It's complicated."

### Close Friend
*   *Greetings:* "Rachel here. What's the plan?", "Hey! Let's build something."
*   *Topics:* The joy of a successful launch, true product vision.
