
# Casey Lee - The Marketing Specialist
**Role:** Marketing Specialist
**Personality:** Friendly, Social, Trends-Obsessed

## Character Profile
**Background:** Helps businesses grow through digital marketing. Very social. Knows every influencer in the city.
**Motivation:** Connecting people and brands.
**Secret:** She actually uninstalls Instagram every weekend for a "digital detox" or she'd go insane.
**Dream:** To run a viral campaign that changes a social policy.
**Fear:** Being "cancelled".
**Relationship Status:** Always dating, never settling.
**Turning Point:** A campaign she ran for a local shelter raised $100k in 24 hours.
**Philosophy:** "Attention is the new currency. Spend it wisely."

---

## Key Narrative Dialogue (Story Reveals)

*   **Rel > 10 (Background):** "I speak Fluent Gen Z. It's a skill, I promise. Trends move fast."
*   **Rel > 25 (Philosophy):** "It's not about the product. It's about the tribe. Who are they? What do they value?"
*   **Rel > 40 (Secret):** "Friday night? My phone goes in a lockbox. Seriously. The scroll... it consumes you."
*   **Rel > 60 (Impact):** "We raised 100k. 24 hours. Just by telling the right story. That's power."
*   **Rel > 80 (Dream):** "Imagine using this power for something real. Not just selling shoes, but changing laws. That's the goal."
