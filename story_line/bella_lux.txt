
# Bella Lux - The Influencer
**Role:** Influencer
**Personality:** High Maintenance, Charismatic, Materialistic

## Character Profile
**Background:** Always chasing the spotlight. Wants a partner who can fund her lifestyle. 
**Motivation:** Fame. Likes. Views.
**Secret:** It's all fake. The leased car, the rented clothes. She's drowning in debt.
**Dream:** To be famous for being famous.
**Fear:** Irrelevance.
**Relationship Status:** "Single" (for the engagement).
**Turning Point:** Her first viral video. She got addicted to the dopamine hit.
**Philosophy:** "Fake it 'til you make it. Then fake it some more."

---

## Key Narrative Dialogue (Story Reveals)

*   **Rel > 10 (Background):** "Do you know who I am? I have 2 million followers. That's basically royalty."
*   **Rel > 25 (Philosophy):** "Perception is reality. If it looks expensive, it is expensive."
*   **Rel > 40 (Secret):** "This bag? Rented. The car? Leased. But the likes? Real. And that's all that matters."
*   **Rel > 60 (Fear):** "If I stop posting, do I disappear? Sometimes I think I might."
*   **Rel > 80 (Vulnerability):** "Maybe I want something real. Just one thing not for the 'gram. Could that be you?"
