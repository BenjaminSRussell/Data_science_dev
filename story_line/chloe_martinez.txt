
# Chloe Martinez - The Rival Analyst
**Role:** Rival Analyst
**Personality:** Competitive, Sharp, Unapologetic

## Character Profile
**Background:** Started around the same time as you. Always trying to steal your clients. Top of her class at university.
**Motivation:** Being Number One.
**Secret:** She admires your work ethic, but sees it as a threat she must neutralize.
**Dream:** To be the CEO of a data empire.
**Fear:** Mediocrity.
**Relationship Status:** Married to her ambition.
**Turning Point:** Losing a major competition in college. She vowed never to lose again.
**Philosophy:** "There is no second place. You win, or you learn."

---

## Key Narrative Dialogue (Story Reveals)

*   **Rel > 10 (Background):** "I graduated Summa Cum Laude. You? Just checking the competition."
*   **Rel > 25 (Philosophy):** "Clients don't want 'good'. They want the best. That's me."
*   **Rel > 40 (Secret Respect):** "I saw your latest report. Not terrible. I would have used a different model, but... decent."
*   **Rel > 60 (Drive):** "I don't sleep. I optimize. That's why I'll always be one step ahead."
*   **Rel > 80 (Goal):** "CEO. That's the title I want. And I'm not asking for permission."
