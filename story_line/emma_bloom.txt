
# Emma Bloom - Librarian & Researcher
**Age:** 28
**Role:** Librarian
**Personality:** Friendly, Quiet, Thoughtful

## Character Profile
**Background:** Quiet librarian who loves books and data. Has a master's in library science and a passion for information architecture. Spends her free time reading research papers.
**Motivation:** Believes that knowledge should be accessible to everyone. Sees herself as a bridge between information and people.
**Secret:** She's actually published three research papers under a pseudonym. She's too shy to claim them publicly, but she's proud of her work.
**Dream:** To digitize rare historical documents and make them searchable using AI. Wants to preserve history for future generations.
**Fear:** That she's wasting her potential, that she should be doing more, that she's too quiet to make a real impact.
**Relationship Status:** Single, lives alone with her cat. Has a few close friends, but prefers books to people most of the time.
**Turning Point:** When a student thanked her for helping them find the perfect research paper. That moment made her realize her work matters.
**Philosophy:** "Information is power, but only if it's accessible. My job is to make sure everyone can find what they need."

## Story Phases & Choices

### Phase 1: The Intellectual Connection
**Trigger:** Relationship Level 15
**Context:** Emma shares her private reading habits with the player.
**Dialogue:** "I... I have something to show you. These are some papers I've been reading. I don't usually share them."

**Player Options:**
1.  **Academic Support:** "I'd love to read your research papers properly."
    *   *Effect:* Intelligence +5, Relationship +5
    *   *Result:* She feels validated intellectually.
2.  **Personal Support:** "You have so much to offer, Emma. You shouldn't stay hidden."
    *   *Effect:* Relationship +10
    *   *Result:* She feels seen as a person.
3.  **Library Visionary:** "This library is a treasure trove. We could modernize it."
    *   *Effect:* Ethics +5, Relationship +5
    *   *Result:* You bond over a shared vision.

### Phase 2: The Secret Revealed
**Trigger:** Relationship Level 35 & Phase 1 Complete
**Context:** A journal wants to interview her pseudonym, 'E.B.'. She faces a crisis of identity.
**Dialogue:** "I have to make a choice. A journal wants to interview 'E.B.' about my latest paper. They don't know it's me."

**Player Options:**
1.  **The Ghostwriter:** "Your research is vital. Keep publishing anonymously."
    *   *Effect:* Ethics +5, Relationship +10
    *   *Result:* She stays comfortable but successful in secret.
2.  **The Spotlight:** "It's time the world knew who 'E.B.' really is. You deserve the credit."
    *   *Effect:* Charisma/Karma +10, Relationship +5
    *   *Result:* She steps into the public eye (terrifying but rewarding).
3.  **The Collaborator:** "Let's work together. My data science, your history."
    *   *Effect:* Intelligence +5, Relationship +15
    *   *Result:* You form a powerful professional partnership.

### Phase 3: The Digitization Project
**Trigger:** Relationship Level 55 & Phase 2 Complete
**Context:** The project to digitize history is approved. You must decide its direction.
**Dialogue:** "The project has been approved! We're starting the digitization. But I need your advice on the priority."

**Player Options:**
1.  **Efficiency Focus:** "Focus on speed and efficiency. Get it done fast."
    *   *Effect:* Logic +5, Relationship +5
2.  **Human/Context Focus:** "Preserve the notes and human touches. The context."
    *   *Effect:* Ethics +5, Relationship +10
3.  **Access Focus:** "Make it free and accessible to everyone."
    *   *Effect:* Reputation +10, Relationship +5

### Phase 4: The Final Choice
**Trigger:** Relationship Level 80 & Phase 3 Complete
**Context:** Success leads to a career crossroads.
**Dialogue:** "It's done. The project is a success. And... I've been offered a position at the University. Or I could stay."

**Player Options:**
1.  **The Professor:** "Take the University job. You're a professor at heart."
    *   *Final Outcome:* Professor Bloom.
2.  **The Tech Librarian:** "Stay here. Make this library the city's info hub."
    *   *Final Outcome:* Tech Librarian.
3.  **The Partner:** "Whatever you do, I want to be by your side."
    *   *Final Outcome:* Life Partner.
