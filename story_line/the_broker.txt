
# Gordon "The Broker" - The Insider
**Role:** Stock Operator (Criminal)
**Personality:** Greedy, Sophisticated, Corrupt

## Character Profile
**Background:** Former Wall Street legend, now operating in the shadows after a "misunderstanding" with the SEC.
**Motivation:** Proving he's smarter than the regulators.
**Secret:** He lost his license years ago. Every trade he makes is through a proxy.
**Dream:** To crash a market just to prove he can.
**Fear:** Being ignored.
**Relationship Status:** Divorced. His assets are in holding companies.
**Turning Point:** The 2008 crash. He made a fortune while the world burned.
**Philosophy:** "Greed is good? No. Greed is necessary."

---

## Key Narrative Dialogue (Story Reveals)

*   **Rel > 10 (Background):** "I used to ring the opening bell. Now I make sure it stops ringing. The shadows are more profitable."
*   **Rel > 25 (Philosophy):** "Information isn't free. Alpha costs money. Or silence. Can you afford silence?"
*   **Rel > 40 (Secret):** "They took my license. They thought that would stop me. It only made me invisible."
*   **Rel > 60 (Goal):** "A flash crash. A momentary blip. Billions wiped out. And us? We catch the falling knives."
*   **Rel > 80 (Hubris):** "I am the market. The market is me. It breathes when I tell it to."
