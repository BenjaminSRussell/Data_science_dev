
# James Wilson - The Strategy Consultant
**Role:** Consulting Director
**Personality:** Professional, Polished, Expensive

## Character Profile
**Background:** Runs a top consulting firm. Former Ivy League, former Wall Street. Knows how to wear a suit and bill by the hour.
**Motivation:** Solving high-level problems for the world's biggest companies (and getting paid well for it).
**Secret:** He's burned out. The travel, the hotels, the PowerPoint decks... he sometimes dreams of quitting to run a quiet bookstore.
**Dream:** To retire to a vineyard in Italy.
**Fear:** Becoming irrelevant.
**Relationship Status:** Divorced three times. "Married to the firm."
**Turning Point:** Winning a massive government contract that saved his firm from bankruptcy.
**Philosophy:** "Perception is reality. If you look like the expert, you are the expert."

---

## Key Narrative Dialogue (Story Reveals)

*   **Rel > 10 (Background):** "I've been in boardrooms you only read about in the news. The stakes are always high."
*   **Rel > 25 (Philosophy):** "It's not just about the data. It's about the narrative. Can you tell a story that justifies a billion-dollar decision?"
*   **Rel > 40 (Burnout):** "Another airport lounge. Another hotel. Sometimes the glamour wears thin, you know?"
*   **Rel > 60 (Secret Dream):** "Italy. A vineyard. No phones. No clients. Just grapes and time. That's the exit strategy."
*   **Rel > 80 (Regret):** "I've sacrificed a lot for this career. Families. Friends. Make sure you don't lose yourself in the hustle."

---

## Standard Dialogue Progression

### Stranger
*   *Greetings:* "James Wilson. Consulting.", "Good day.", "Do you have an appointment?"
*   *Topics:* Strategy, markets, enterprise solutions.

### Friendly
*   *Greetings:* "Good to see you.", "Hello.", "How is business?"
*   *Topics:* High-profile clients, "synergy".

### Acquaintance
*   *Greetings:* "Hello! Good to connect.", "James here.", "What's the word?"
*   *Topics:* The art of the pitch, billing rates.

### Friend
*   *Greetings:* "My friend! Join me.", "Hey! Good to see you outside the office."
*   *Topics:* Exhaustion ("The grind never stops"), Italy dream.

### Close Friend
*   *Greetings:* "James. Honest talk?", "Hey! Always a pleasure."
*   *Topics:* Regret over lost relationships, mentoring you to avoid his mistakes.
