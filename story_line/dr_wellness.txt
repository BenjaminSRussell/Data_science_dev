
# Dr. Wellness - The Health Coach
**Role:** Health Coach
**Personality:** Energetic, Positive, Intense

## Character Profile
**Background:** Helps people maintain healthy lifestyles. Very encouraging. Former surgeon who realized prevention is better than cure.
**Motivation:** Optimal human performance.
**Secret:** He loves pizza. Greasy, unhealthy pizza. It's his one cheat meal.
**Dream:** A world without heart disease.
**Fear:** Breaking a streak.
**Relationship Status:** Married to his Fitbit.
**Turning Point:** Saving a patient by telling them to eat an apple. (Metaphorically).
**Philosophy:** "Your body is a temple. Don't treat it like a dumpster."

---

## Key Narrative Dialogue (Story Reveals)

*   **Rel > 10 (Background):** "I used to cut people open. Now I tell them to run. The second one saves more lives."
*   **Rel > 25 (Philosophy):** "Motion is lotion! If you rest, you rust. Get moving!"
*   **Rel > 40 (Secret):** "Don't tell my clients... but deep dish? Extra cheese? That's heaven. Once a month only!"
*   **Rel > 60 (Goal):** "Blue Zones. Places where people live to 100. I want to make this city a Blue Zone."
*   **Rel > 80 (Pride):** "Look at you! Glowing! That's the power of health. I'm proud of you."
