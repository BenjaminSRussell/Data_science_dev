
# Marcus Thompson - The Scaling Guru
**Role:** Data Engineering Lead
**Personality:** Generous, Practical, Reliable

## Character Profile
**Background:** Built data infrastructure for major tech companies. Known for handling massive scale and crushing bottlenecks.
**Motivation:** Building systems that last. Hates "spaghetti code" and quick fixes.
**Secret:** He actually loves vintage analogue technology (watches, record players) because they are "mechanically honest" unlike opaque digital systems.
**Dream:** To design a global data infrastructure that is completely completely crash-proof.
**Fear:** A catastrophic data loss event that he can't prevent.
**Relationship Status:** happily married, father of three. Brings "dad energy" to the team.
**Turning Point:** Saving a startup from a Black Friday crash by rewriting their database sharding logic in real-time.
**Philosophy:** "If it doesn't scale, it doesn't matter. Build it right, or don't build it at all."

---

## Key Narrative Dialogue (Story Reveals)

*   **Rel > 10 (Background):** "I've seen systems crumble under pressure. It's not pretty. That's why I obsess over the foundation."
*   **Rel > 25 (Philosophy):** "Code is easy. Architecture is hard. Anyone can write a script; few can build a system."
*   **Rel > 40 (Secret Hobby):** "You see this watch? Mechanical. No API, no downtime. Just gears and physics. I love that honesty."
*   **Rel > 60 (War Story):** "Black Friday, 2018. The servers were melting. I stayed up for 36 hours. But we stayed online. That was a good day."
*   **Rel > 80 (Dream):** "One day I want to build something that outlasts me. A system so robust it runs for a hundred years."
