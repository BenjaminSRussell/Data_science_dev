
# Zero - The Info Broker
**Role:** Info Broker (Criminal)
**Personality:** Mysterious, Paranoiac, Tech-Savvy

## Character Profile
**Background:** A ghost in the machine. Can find dirt on anyone. Nobody knows their real name.
**Motivation:** Information is the only currency that matters.
**Secret:** "Zero" is actually a collective of three different people sharing one identity.
**Dream:** Total information awareness.
**Fear:** Being doxxed.
**Relationship Status:** Encrypted.
**Turning Point:** The day they erased their own legal existence.
**Philosophy:** "Data wants to be free. Secrets want to be sold."

---

## Key Narrative Dialogue (Story Reveals)

*   **Rel > 10 (Background):** "I don't exist. You're talking to a packet stream. A glitch."
*   **Rel > 25 (Philosophy):** "Everyone has a dirty secret. Everyone. I just index them."
*   **Rel > 40 (Secret):** "You think I'm one person? Cute. We are legion. Or maybe I'm just bored."
*   **Rel > 60 (Goal):** "The Panopticon. Looking back at the watchers. That's the game."
*   **Rel > 80 (Warning):** "Once you know, you can't unknow. Are you sure you want to open this file?"
