
# Priya Sharma - The Ethics Officer
**Role:** Data Privacy Officer
**Personality:** Professional, Principled, Stern but Fair

## Character Profile
**Background:** Former human rights lawyer who pivoted to tech policy. Now ensures companies follow data privacy laws.
**Motivation:** Protecting people from the misuse of their personal data.
**Secret:** She once had to whistleblower on a previous employer, a traumatic event that made her the strict enforcer she is today.
**Dream:** A global "Digital Bill of Rights" that is universally enforced.
**Fear:** A surveillance state where privacy no longer exists.
**Relationship Status:** Protecting her own privacy fiercely.
**Turning Point:** The "Whistleblower Incident" - realizing that laws weren't enough, and that someone had to stand up from the inside.
**Philosophy:** "Privacy isn't a luxury. It's a fundamental human right."

---

## Key Narrative Dialogue (Story Reveals)

*   **Rel > 10 (Background):** "I used to represent people, not policies. But I realized the biggest threat to rights was in the code, not the courts."
*   **Rel > 25 (Philosophy):** "Just because you *can* collect the data, doesn't mean you *should*. That distinction is everything."
*   **Rel > 40 (Secret Past):** "I learned the hard way that doing the right thing has a cost. I paid it once. I'd pay it again."
*   **Rel > 60 (Fear):** "We are sleepwalking into a world where everything is known, tracked, and sold. I'm just trying to wake us up."
*   **Rel > 80 (Dream):** "A Digital Bill of Rights. Enforceable. Global. That's the only way to ensure freedom in the digital age."
